I’m a Mathematics student at UT Austin, graduating in December 2026. I plan to pursue graduate studies in quantitative finance and related fields.

        
Over the past two years I’ve worked in a few corners of quantitative finance and data. At Guangzhou Futures I spent a summer on factor research for commodity futures and on a risk dashboard for live accounts. Before that I worked on data quality at China Everbright Securities and Guangdong Bozhong, checking and reconciling financial records before anyone modelled them.

        
I’m drawn to quantitative research, especially trading strategies, portfolio risk, and model validation. Most of my work comes back to one question: how far can a model’s output be trusted?

        
The projects on this site are where I test that question on my own time. Most of them are an excuse to learn a method properly and see whether it holds up on real data, and each page says what the analysis cannot show.

Earlier, I competed in DECA and finished in the top 1% at its international conference.

        
My goal is to become a quantitative researcher, turning rigorous analysis into informed investment decisions. If you work on anything related to markets, I’d be glad to hear from you.
